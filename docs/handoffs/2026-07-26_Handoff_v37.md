## Decisions taken

1. **The capstone is the one cue louder than a stranding.** The teeth principle
   is about what the player meets repeatedly, and breaking the blockade happens
   once in a playthrough. The test that pinned "loudest cue on the moment that
   hurt" now names this exception rather than filtering it silently, so a second
   one has to argue for itself.
2. **A bonus delivery is a brighter delivery, not a layer.** #384 asked for a
   flourish over the delivery cue; one voice per frame makes that exactly the pile
   the rule prevents. `delivered-bonus` reaches an octave higher instead. Coins
   gained stay folded into the delivery for the same reason.
3. **Ticks sit inside the bed's range, not above it.** A tick is heard because it
   is a short pitched transient against broadband noise, not because it out-shouts
   the wheels. This inverted an assumption written into the first draft of the
   tier comments, and a test caught it.
4. **The frame flush lives at the top of `update()`.** It is the only statement
   that provably runs on every path through a frame, including both modal
   early-returns. The cue a frame earns is therefore heard one frame later, which
   is inaudible; a missed early-return would not be.
5. **Two call sites request and flush in the same breath**: region travel and new
   game. Both call `scene.restart`/`scene.start`, which replaces the Audio before
   the next frame arrives. Everywhere else the top-of-frame flush is enough.
6. **The e2e hook grew two logs, and they are different questions.** "Did this
   call site fire" and "what did the player hear" stopped being the same thing the
   moment cues started sharing frames: the first delivery of a run requests the
   delivery cue and is heard as the achievement it earned. A pre-existing
   assertion in `audio.spec.ts` was quietly measuring the wrong one and started
   failing, correctly.
7. **Scroll and fog reveal stay silent**, recorded as decisions rather than
   omissions. Scroll fires per wheel notch and would buzz; the bed already carries
   the sense of motion that a fog cue would duplicate.

## Verification notes

- **Forty-one guards were neutralized and watched to fail** across the three PRs.
  Three worth keeping:
  - Bypassing the master gain left every test green. Testing `synthesizeCue` in
    isolation proves a cue *can* be pointed at a master gain, not that the one the
    game plays through is. That is trap 1's function-with-no-caller in its
    quietest costume: the sound still comes out, it just cannot be turned down.
    The output graph builder is now exported and the routing asserted directly.
  - Removing the explicit flush before `scene.restart` fails the travel spec.
    That was the line most likely to be silently wrong and it now has the loudest
    test.
  - Removing the `announce` gate on the region-cleared cue fails with "boot into
    a cleared region should be silent". Without it, every reload of a finished
    region congratulates the player.
- **The neutralize/restore tooling corrupted the source once.** It anchored on
  `this.refreshSkillPanel();`, which appears twice at the same indentation, so a
  restore put a cue call in the success branch of `handleSkillInput` instead of
  the refusal branch. Caught by re-reading the diff, not by a test, because a test
  was green either way: the wrong branch still fires *a* cue, and nothing asserts
  what a successful rank does not sound like. Trap 1's point arriving from the
  tooling side.
- **The bed spec flaked twice on `page.keyboard.press('V')`.** A zero-gap keypress
  falls between starved frames, the mute never happens, and the failure reads as
  "the wheels kept rolling while muted", which points at the wrong subsystem
  entirely. Switched to `tapKey` plus a poll, then sampled four consecutive clean
  runs. Trap 4's first face in a new place.
- **The mix was rendered through a real `OfflineAudioContext`** after each PR, the
  method #382 established. Final state: all 37 cues render without throwing, every
  peak within 20% of 0.9x its table gain (ratios 0.88 to 1.00), audible spans
  62-71% of nominal duration, bed plus the loudest cue peaking at 0.251 against
  0.255 for the cue alone, so nothing clips.
- **Every PR ran the full browser suite**, not just the arc.

## Risks or decisions needed

- **The bed is about three times quieter than its numbers read.** It peaks at
  0.021 to 0.030 while commanding up to 0.08, because bandpass-filtered noise
  carries far less peak energy than its envelope ceiling. The commanded numbers
  are the ones #383 specified and the ones the tests pin, so they were kept, but
  this is the single most likely thing to be wrong when a human listens. Raising
  it is one constant.
- **Nobody has heard any of this at volume.** Thirty-seven cues were reasoned,
  tiered, and measured; none has been judged. Retuning now is rows of numbers in
  `src/systems/audio-cues.ts`. After Phase 2 swaps in samples it is an asset job.
  This is the same risk v36 raised with eight cues, and it is now four times
  larger.
- **The one-cue-per-frame rule is doing more than it looks.** Several moments now
  routinely lose their frame: the first delivery of a run is heard as an
  achievement, a delivery that clears a region is heard as the clear, and arriving
  somewhere new to collect cargo is heard as the arrival. Each of those is a
  defensible call, but they were decided by two numbers in a table rather than by
  anyone listening to the sequence.
- **`map-scene.ts` grew by ~450 lines and nothing tracks it.** It is now past the
  2326-line peak that prompted the last refactor. Third
  handoff running.
- **Release drift is now seven PRs deep.**
