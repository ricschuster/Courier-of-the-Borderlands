## What changed and why

Slice 5 (v46) put Reedgrave at 167% of a Demanding starting tank, so stranding
there stopped being an edge case and became that preset's normal first lesson. At
`limpSpeed: 0.1` the recovery crawl then cost more real minutes than anything
else in the game.

Wear rate, tank size and repair price are untouched, so the wagon breaks exactly
as often and needs the shop exactly as often. Only the price of recovery moved,
and it is paid in the one currency the game cannot refund. Failure must be cheap
for the soft gate to teach rather than punish (#433), and Demanding is the preset
where players fail most.

## The finding: the fix did not move the number

Re-measured after the change:

```
MEASURE_DIFF=demanding npx playwright test travel-sink-measure
Test timeout of 720000ms exceeded, at drive.ts:212 (readTick, inside driveToTile)
Slow test file: 12.0m
```

Identical to before: killed mid-drive, no stall watchdog trip, no `courier
stuck`, step budget not exhausted. A 1.5x quicker crawl was not the binding
constraint, which means the diagnosis in #448 was wrong about the cause even
though the change is defensible on its own terms.

### Revised diagnosis, still a hypothesis

Demanding's tank starts at 16 and grows 9 per level. Reedgrave costs 26.7 wear
one way on a bare wagon, so:

| courier | tank | one way | round trip |
|---|---|---|---|
| level 1, bare | 16 | no | no |
| level 3, bare | 34 | yes | no |
| level 2, + axle | 25 | yes (20.0) | no |
| level 3, + axle + treads | 34 | yes (13.4) | yes (26.7) |

On Demanding, Reedgrave is a **level-3, two-upgrades destination**. That is the
Gothic model working as `10_open_world_expansion.md` describes it: see the far
place, fail, come back stronger.

The scripted driver has no "come back later" behaviour. It takes the board's
first available contract and drives at it, so it walks into a leg it cannot
finish at level 1 and pays in crawl time, repeatedly. A person reads the same
board and does the road runs first.

If that is right, the arc timing out on Demanding is a property of the
measurement tool and not of the game, and the tool (or its budget) is the thing
to change. If it is wrong, Demanding's early game genuinely is a crawl and the
tank or the hub multiplier is the lever. **Nothing measured so far separates
those two**, which is why the issue is open rather than closed.

## Decisions

- **Do not close an issue on a change that did not move its number.** The limp
  bump was asked for, is landed, and is right on its own terms. It is not a fix
  for "the measured run no longer finishes", and saying otherwise would have left
  a false negative in the tracker for whoever picks #448 up next.
- **Remove an override rather than set it to the inherited value.** A preset that
  duplicates standard's number silently stops tracking it.
